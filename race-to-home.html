Race to Home

A pencil-and-paper race for 2 to 4 players

Tap a creature to switch between You, Computer and Off.

House rules

Every family plays a little differently. Pick yours.

How to play

  1. Start. Every creature begins on its own X. Each player has 4.
  2. Move. Roll the die and move one creature that many squares: counter-clockwise around the outside, then around the inside ring and into Home.
  3. Stay safe. The X squares and Home are safe. Anywhere else, if someone lands on you, you go back to your X.
  4. Pairs. Two or more of your creatures on the same square make a pair, and a pair can't be sent back.
  5. Lanes. Each color's route is drawn on the board. The brighter, moving one belongs to whoever's turn it is.
  6. Extra rolls. Roll again after every 6, every time you send someone back, and every time a creature reaches Home.
  7. Exact roll. You need the exact number to reach Home. If nothing can move, the turn passes.
  8. Skip. Don't like any move, say one that walks into danger? Tap "Skip turn" after rolling. A skipped 6 still gets its extra roll.
  9. Win. First to get all 4 Home wins. Everyone else plays on for 2nd and 3rd.

These are the default rules. Change them in House rules on the start screen. Tip: tap any creature to say hi.
